About The Platform

PBL Project Overview

The Smart Academic and Career Intelligence Platform was developed by Team G-2 to address the uncertainty university students face when transitioning from academic coursework to professional careers.

Rather than relying on uninterpretable black-box models or generic personality tests, our platform implements a deterministic, rule-based recommendation engine. By evaluating concrete competencies against curated industry profiles, it produces reproducible, verifiable career match scores.

Academic Note: Match scores represent deterministic suitability metrics based on current qualifications and provide clear paths for improvement.

Recommendation Scoring Model

Deterministic Formula

Our engine applies a transparent, weighted multi-factor formula to compute your Career Match Score:

50%
Skills Matching: Evaluates your verified technical and soft skills against core role requisites.
30%
Interests Alignment: Measures domain curiosity and focus areas to ensure occupational longevity.
20%
Academic Performance: Normalizes current CGPA on a standard 10.0 scale for baseline rigor.
